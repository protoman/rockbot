This list comes from three sources. First, what I confirmed against the PC source today. Second, the port's maintained gap notes (pc_gaps.md, bugs.md). Third, the older pc_features_vs_md.md. That last file is partly stale. It still lists the AI jump family and BUG-018 as open, which later notes show are done. So I've split the list by how sure I am.

Confirmed today, still lacking

1. Room slide, incoming objects: the PC also draws the incoming map's objects, enemies and above-player layer during the slide. The port shows only the tiles, and the rest pop in after the slide.
2. Room slide, incoming background: at 12 links, including CHAOS CITY map 0 to 1, the incoming parallax becomes plain sky, because the background palettes differ and PAL3 is shared.
3. Slide timing: 8 px per frame in the port, against the PC's 4 px per step with delays. The PC also pauses 100 ms after arrival.
4. Boss door opening: the PC code (redraw_boss_door) retracts the door's tiles one per 100 ms, bottom first, and closes top-down. The port opens centre-out at about 50 ms per slice. That matches your recollection of the PC but not the code I read, so this one needs your call.
5. Charge indicator: the PC cycles three colours every 200 ms, and every 100 ms when fully charged. The port strobes two states at 8 and 2 frames, which is a palette limit.
6. Wall-shooter missile art: the aim logic matches the PC. The missile sprite isn't rotated, since the port has no image rotation.
